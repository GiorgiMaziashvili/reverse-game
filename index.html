Add Reverse Audio to your Home Screen, then launch it from its icon for an app window without the browser address bar.

iPhone / iPad
Open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Turn on “Open as Web App” if shown, then tap Add.

Android
Open in Chrome → ⋮ → Install app or Add to Home screen → Install.

Use the published HTTPS website. Installation options depend on your browser. Your phone may ask for microphone permission again.
